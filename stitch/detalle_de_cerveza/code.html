The Heritage

"A velvet whirlwind of nitrogen and tradition."

Famous for its smooth, creamy head and dark, rich body, Guinness Draught is the definitive Irish stout. Since 1759, the brewery at St. James's Gate has mastered the balance of roasted barley and hops.

The profile features distinct notes of coffee and dark chocolate, achieved through a unique roasting process that gives the beer its iconic ruby-red hue (often mistaken for black).
